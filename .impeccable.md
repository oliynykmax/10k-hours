## Design Context

### Users
Anyone tracking progress toward skill mastery. The app is built around the 10,000-hour rule — deliberate practice toward expertise. Users want to see their accumulated practice time, hit milestones, and maintain daily streaks.

### Brand Personality
Warm, motivating, grounded — "Catppuccin warmth meets mastery tracking"

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Light theme**: Catppuccin Latte — cream base, lavender primary, peach accent
- **Dark theme**: Catppuccin Mocha — deep base, lavender primary, peach accent
- **Typography**: Clash Display for headings, Satoshi for body
- **Feel**: Clean and approachable. The timer should feel like a companion for long practice sessions, not a pressure tool. Milestones and streaks provide gentle motivation.
- **Key moments**: Starting a practice session (peach highlight glow), hitting a milestone (progress bar fills), calendar filling up (streak visualization)

### Design Principles
1. **Practice is the core** — the timer is the hero. Everything else supports it.
2. **Progress is visible** — milestones, streaks, and total hours are always in view
3. **Low friction** — start/stop practicing in one tap, everything local-first
4. **Warm but focused** — Catppuccin palette provides warmth without distraction
5. **Gamified gently** — milestones reward consistency, not competition
